Mam-IA is the shared brain of your family: everything the household has to remember, in one place, for every adult in the family.

• Calendar: school, activities, doctors, birthdays… who goes, who drives and who picks up. Reminders on your phone before each plan.
• Tasks and routines: chores that repeat and rotate between you, so the load is shared fairly. See who did what this week.
• Shopping lists: the supermarket and the pharmacy, sorted by aisle, updated live for everyone.
• Weekly menu: plan dinners and school lunches and send the ingredients to the shopping list.
• Kids' profiles: school, teacher, schedule, allergies, sizes and useful contacts, always at hand.
• Daily summary and reminders by email, if you want them.

Invite your partner or anyone who helps at home: everyone sees the same thing at the same time. Works on Android and on the web, in 10 languages.
